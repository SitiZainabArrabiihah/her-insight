🌸 Her Insight — Reproductive Health Education Platform for Visually Impaired Women
> An inclusive, audio-first assistive software prototype aimed at bridging the gap in reproductive health education and digital health literacy for visually impaired women.

💡 Overview & Problem Statement
Reproductive health resources often rely heavily on visual graphics, making them inaccessible to visually impaired individuals. Her Insight addresses this disparity by delivering an accessible, privacy-focused educational environment tailored to the unique learning and accessibility needs of visually impaired women.

🌟 Key Features
-Audio-First Interactive Modules:Navigable lessons on anatomy, menstrual hygiene, and bodily health designed specifically for screen readers.
-Inclusive Accessibility UX:High-contrast interface, voice-guided interactions, and screen-reader compatibility (TalkBack/VoiceOver).
-Private & Taboo-Free Learning: A secure platform that empowers users to learn sensitive health information independently and confidentially.

🎯 Target UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
-SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being
-SDG 5: Gender Equality
-SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

🛠️ Tech Stack & Methodology
-Core Framework: Python & HTML-CSS-JS
-Accessibility StandardWCAG 2.1 Guidelines (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)
-Research Base: Field research and user testing at special education schools (SLB).
